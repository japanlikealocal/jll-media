At a Japanese konbini? Here's what the staff will ask you 🏪 Save this for your trip 📌

1 "Atatamemasu ka?" – Shall I heat it up? Bento, noodles and many ready meals can be heated in the store. Yes: Hai, onegaishimasu · No: Daijoubu desu
2 "Fukuro irimasu ka?" – Do you need a bag? Plastic bags are paid since July 2020, usually a few yen (each store sets the price).
3 "Ohashi otsuke shimasu ka?" – Do you need chopsticks? At some stores you take cutlery yourself.
4 Paying – big chains take IC cards (Suica, PASMO) and major credit cards. A few items are exceptions.
5 Need cash? – Seven Bank ATMs (in 7-Eleven) accept many overseas cards, in 12 languages. Some cards may not work; fees vary.
6 Where's the trash? – street bins are rare (the #1 problem visitors report in the Japan Tourism Agency survey). Carry a small bag, and don't bring trash from home to store bins.

General tips, checked Nov 2026 (7-Eleven, Seven Bank, METI, Japan Tourism Agency). Varies by store.
