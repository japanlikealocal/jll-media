Hungry in Japan but don't speak Japanese? 🍜

3 things to know:
1. Wait to be seated. Staff may ask "How many?" — hold up fingers
2. Some ramen shops use ticket machines. Buy a ticket first, then hand it to staff
3. Point at photos or food models. Ask: "Eigo no menyuu wa arimasu ka?" (Do you have an English menu?)

No tipping needed. Rules vary by restaurant, so follow staff instructions.
