First ramen in Japan? Here's how the ticket machine works 🎫🍜 Save this for your trip 📌

1 Buy before you sit – at most ramen shops you pay first at the machine, usually near the door. Some shops take orders from staff.
2 Have cash ready – cash still works best. More machines now take IC cards (Suica) and QR. ¥1,000 bills and coins are the safe bet.
3 Press your bowl – put money in, then press the button for your ramen, drinks or sides.
4 Extras = more tickets – toppings or a large size often have their own buttons.
5 Hand the ticket over – take your change and give the ticket to staff. Some shops ask you to leave it on the counter.
6 Noodle firmness? – some shops let you choose, on a sheet or by asking. Katame = firm · Futsuu = regular · Yawarakame = soft.

General tips, checked Nov 2026 (JNTO and shop sites). Every shop is a little different.
