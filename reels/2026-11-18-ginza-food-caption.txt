Hungry in Ginza? 🍱 5 food spots near the station — save this for your Tokyo trip 📌

1 Ginza Tenryu (銀座 天龍 本店) – Ginza-itchome, direct. Pan-fried gyoza ¥1,330 (tax incl.). Cash & cards.
2 Takahashiya (高橋屋) – Higashi-Ginza, 1 min. Unagi. English menu. Unadon set ¥5,800.
3 Hyotanya (ひょうたん屋 6丁目店) – Higashi-Ginza, 2 min. Unagi. English menu. Cash only.
4 Ginza Hachigo (銀座 八五) – Higashi-Ginza, 3 min. Michelin-listed ramen. Book on TableCheck.
5 Torigin (鳥ぎん 銀座本店) – Ginza, 1 min. Yakitori 5-skewer set ¥1,240. Cash only, walk-in.
