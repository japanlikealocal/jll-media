First train ride in Japan? 🚃

3 things to know:
1. Tap your IC card (Suica, PASMO, etc.) when you enter and exit
2. Line up where marked and let people off first
3. Set your phone to silent, avoid calls and keep your voice down

Rules may vary by line and station.
