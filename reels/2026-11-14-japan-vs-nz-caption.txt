Visiting Japan from New Zealand? 🇯🇵🇳🇿 10 everyday differences to know before you land 📌

1 Tipping – Japan: not expected · NZ: not required (optional for great service)
2 Price tags – Japan: tax included (if two prices are shown, look for the tax-in one) · NZ: prices usually include 15% GST; check for extra charges
3 Driving side – Japan: left · NZ: drive on the left
4 Escalators – Japan: stand still, don't walk (Tokyo: often stand left) · NZ: people tend to stand left; follow local signs
5 Shoes at home – Japan: off at the door · NZ: ask your host before going in
6 Calls on trains – Japan: avoided on local trains · NZ: keep calls quiet, no speakerphone
7 Paying at restaurants – Japan: often at the register (varies by shop) · NZ: some cafés take orders and payment at the counter; check when you go in
8 Greeting – Japan: often a bow · NZ: handshake common when first meeting (not required)
9 Hot spring / spa – Japan: usually no swimsuit · NZ: public hot pools: swimwear; private pools: check the rules
10 Emergency – Japan: 110 police, 119 fire/ambulance · NZ: 111 for police, fire and ambulance

General customs — they vary by place, home and business. In Japan, follow local signs and staff instructions.
