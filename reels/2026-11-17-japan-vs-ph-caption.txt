Visiting Japan from the Philippines? 🇯🇵🇵🇭 10 everyday differences to know before you fly 📌

1 Tipping – Japan: not expected · Philippines: often 10% service charge; tip optional if it's already on the bill
2 Price tags – Japan: tax included (if two prices are shown, look for the tax-in one) · Philippines: price tags include 12% VAT by law; restaurants may add a service charge
3 Driving side – Japan: left · Philippines: right
4 Escalators – Japan: stand still, don't walk (Tokyo: often stand left) · Philippines: people tend to stand right (not a rule)
5 Shoes at home – Japan: off at the door · Philippines: often off; follow your host
6 Calls on trains – Japan: avoided on local trains · Philippines: MRT-3 asks riders to keep voices and music down
7 Paying at restaurants – Japan: often at the register (varies by shop) · Philippines: fast food is pay-first at the counter
8 Greeting – Japan: often a bow · Philippines: handshake common
9 Plugs & voltage – Japan: Type A, 100V · Philippines: Types A/B/C, 230V (often called 220V) — check your device's voltage rating
10 Emergency – Japan: 110 police, 119 fire/ambulance · Philippines: 911

General customs — they vary by place, home and business. In Japan, follow local signs and staff instructions.
