Visiting Japan from Australia? Part 2 🇯🇵🇦🇺 9 more things to know before you fly 📌

1 Plugs & voltage – Japan: Type A, 100V · Australia: Type I, 230V (check your charger's voltage rating)
2 Paying – Japan: some small shops and guesthouses are cash only · Australia: cards are the most common way to pay
3 Red light – Japan: don't cross on a red pedestrian light (it's the law) · Australia: same rule in Queensland and Victoria
4 Transit card – Japan: Suica / PASMO (not valid on every line or area) · Australia: Opal in Sydney, myki in Melbourne
5 Tap water – Japan: safe to drink unless a sign says otherwise · Australia: generally safe; follow "not for drinking" signs
6 June–August – Japan: summer · Australia: winter in most areas (the tropical north has wet and dry seasons)
7 Driving in Japan – bring a 1949 Geneva-format International Driving Permit AND your current Australian licence. Your Australian licence alone is not enough. Get the IDP before you fly.
8 Convenience stores – Japan: many open 24h with onigiri, salads and hot meals · Australia: 7-Eleven sells hot food too
9 Eating on the go – Japan: avoided in some areas; follow local signs · Australia (NSW): water only on buses unless medically needed

General info — rules and customs vary by place. In Japan, follow local signs and staff instructions.
