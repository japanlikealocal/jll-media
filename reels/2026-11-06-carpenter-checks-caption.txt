I'm a carpenter for new apartments in Japan 🔨 Here's what gets checked before you get the keys.

1 The door — closes smoothly, no rubbing, no drifting open
2 The gaps — even lines around the door and frame
3 The squeaks — floors, walls, ceilings
4 The shoe closet — adjustable shelves can't wobble
5 The window trim — every screw in place

Then the buyer walks through, lists anything that's off, we fix it, and they check again.

Bonus: most Japanese homes have wallpaper on the walls, not paint. Look closely next time you stay in one 👀
