Visiting Japan from Malaysia? 🇯🇵🇲🇾 10 everyday differences to know before you fly 📌

1 Tipping – Japan: not expected · Malaysia: not required (many mid-range restaurants add a 10% service charge)
2 Price tags – Japan: tax included (if two prices are shown, look for the tax-in one) · Malaysia: many menus add service charge + 6% SST on top
3 Driving side – Japan: left · Malaysia: left too
4 Escalators – Japan: stand still, don't walk (Tokyo: often stand left) · Malaysia: people tend to stand left (not a rule)
5 Shoes at home – Japan: off at the door · Malaysia: off when visiting homes (and at mosques and temples)
6 Calls on trains – Japan: avoided on local trains · Malaysia: MRT/LRT station rules ban making noise
7 Paying at restaurants – Japan: often at the register (varies by shop) · Malaysia: at mamak shops, pay at the counter; at hawker stalls, pay each stall
8 Greeting – Japan: often a bow · Malaysia: handshakes are generally fine; some Muslim women prefer a nod and a smile. Use your right hand.
9 Plugs & voltage – Japan: Type A, 100V · Malaysia: Type G, 240V — bring an adapter and check your device's voltage rating
10 Emergency – Japan: 110 police, 119 fire/ambulance · Malaysia: 999

General customs — they vary by place, home and business. In Japan, follow local signs and staff instructions.
