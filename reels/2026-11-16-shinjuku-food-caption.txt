Hungry in Shinjuku? 🍜 5 food spots near the station — save this for your Tokyo trip 📌

1 Gyoza no Fukuho (餃子の福包 新宿店) – Shinjuku-gyoemmae, 2 min. Pan-fried & boiled gyoza. Cards OK.
2 Rouhen Gyozakan (老辺餃子舘 新宿本店) – Shinjuku South, 1 min. Steamed gyoza, English touch-panel ordering.
3 Fu-unji (新宿 風雲児) – Shinjuku, 1–5 min. Tsukemen ¥1,000. Closes when the soup sells out.
4 Torikizoku (鳥貴族 新宿靖国通り店) – Shinjuku-sanchome, 1 min. All items ¥390 (tax incl.), open late.
5 Torien (鳥園, Omoide Yokocho) – Shinjuku West, 3 min. English menu. ¥330 table charge.

Show the Japanese name to staff if you get lost 🙌
