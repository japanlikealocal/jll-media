Visiting Japan from Singapore? 🇯🇵🇸🇬 10 everyday differences to know before you land 📌

1 Tipping – Japan: not expected · Singapore: tips not expected (many places add a 10% service charge)
2 Price tags – Japan: tax included · Singapore: GST included; check the menu (some restaurants add service charge + GST)
3 Driving side – Japan: left · Singapore: drive on the left
4 Escalators – Tokyo: usually stand left · Singapore: usually left; check signs (some MRT stations are trialling standing on both sides)
5 Shoes at home – Japan: off at the door · Singapore: usually off when visiting homes
6 Calls on trains – Japan: avoided on local trains · Singapore: keep noise low
7 Paying at restaurants – Japan: often at the register · Singapore: ask where to pay (hawker stalls vary)
8 Greeting – Japan: often a bow · Singapore: handshake if offered
9 Plugs & voltage – Japan: Type A plugs, 100V · Singapore: Type G plugs, 230V (check your device's voltage rating)
10 Emergency – Japan: 110 police, 119 fire/ambulance · Singapore: police 999, fire/ambulance 995

General customs — they vary by place, home and business. In Japan, follow local signs and staff instructions.
