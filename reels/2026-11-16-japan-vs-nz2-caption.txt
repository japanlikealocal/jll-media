Visiting Japan from New Zealand? Part 2 🇯🇵🇳🇿 8 more things to know before you fly 📌

1 Plugs & voltage – Japan: Type A, 100V · NZ: Type I, 230/240V (check your charger's voltage rating)
2 Paying – Japan: some small shops and guesthouses are cash only · NZ: cards / EFTPOS are the most common way to pay
3 Red light – Japan: don't cross on a red pedestrian light (it's the law) · NZ: same rule under national road rules
4 Transit card – Japan: Suica / PASMO · NZ: AT HOP in Auckland, Snapper in Wellington (Motu Move is rolling out)
5 Tap water – Japan: safe to drink unless a sign says otherwise · NZ: generally safe; follow any boil-water notice
6 June–August – Japan: summer · NZ: winter
7 Driving in Japan – you need an International Driving Permit (get it from the AA before you fly). Your NZ licence alone, even with a translation, is not enough.
8 Eating on trains – Japan: avoid eating on local trains (shinkansen is fine) · NZ: Auckland trains ask you not to eat; rules vary by city

General info — rules and customs vary by place. In Japan, follow local signs and staff instructions.
