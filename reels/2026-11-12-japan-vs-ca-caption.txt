Visiting Japan from Canada? 🇯🇵🇨🇦 10 everyday differences to know before you land 📌

1 Tipping – Japan: not expected · Canada: 15–20% before tax at sit-down restaurants (check your bill for auto tips)
2 Price tags – Japan: tax included · Canada: tax usually extra (varies by province)
3 Driving side – Japan: left · Canada: drive on the right
4 Escalators – Tokyo: usually stand left · Canada: usually stand right (follow local signs)
5 Shoes at home – Japan: off at the door · Canada: usually off, unless your host says otherwise
6 Calls on trains – Japan: avoided on local trains · Canada: some quiet zones
7 Paying at restaurants – Japan: often at the register · Canada: usually at the table at sit-down restaurants
8 Greeting – Japan: often a bow · Canada: handshakes common (mainly when first meeting)
9 Hot spring / spa – Japan: usually no swimsuit · Canada: usually swimwear (check each venue's rules)
10 Emergency – Japan: 110 police, 119 fire/ambulance · Canada: 911

General customs — they vary by place, home and business. In Japan, follow local signs and staff instructions.
