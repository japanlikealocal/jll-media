Visiting Japan from Australia? 🇯🇵🇦🇺 10 everyday differences to know before you land 📌

1 Tipping – Japan: not expected · Australia: tips not expected
2 Price tags – Japan: tax included · Australia: prices include GST (some cafés add weekend surcharges)
3 Driving side – Japan: left · Australia: drive on the left
4 Escalators – Tokyo: usually stand left · Australia: usually stand left (follow local signs)
5 Shoes at home – Japan: off at the door · Australia: ask your host
6 Calls on trains – Japan: avoided on local trains · Australia: some quiet carriages
7 Paying at restaurants – Japan: often at the register · Australia: ask where to pay
8 Greeting – Japan: often a bow · Australia: handshakes common (mainly when first meeting)
9 Hot spring / spa – Japan: usually no swimsuit · Australia: usually swimwear (check each venue's rules)
10 Emergency – Japan: 110 police, 119 fire/ambulance · Australia: 000

General customs — they vary by place, home and business. In Japan, follow local signs and staff instructions.
