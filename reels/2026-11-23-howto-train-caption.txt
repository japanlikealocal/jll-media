First time on Tokyo trains? Avoid these 6 mistakes 🚇 Save this for your trip 📌

1 Gate won't open – your IC card balance is too low. Use the fare adjustment machine (seisanki) next to the gates. Charging there is cash only.
2 Wrong exit – big stations have many exits. Check the exit number on the yellow signs (Tokyo Metro).
3 Missed the last train – last trains leave between midnight and about 1 a.m., depending on the line. Yamanote Line from Tokyo Station: 0:38 / 0:46.
4 Riding at 8 a.m. – weekday rush is worst around 7:30–8:30. Travel later if you can, especially with big bags.
5 Women-only car – some lines have women-only cars on weekday mornings. Check the platform signs for the car position.
6 Phone near priority seats – when the train is crowded, turn your phone off near priority seats. Elsewhere: silent mode, no calls.

Checked Nov 2026 (JR East, Tokyo Metro, MLIT). Times and rules vary by line, so check Google Maps or the timetable before you go.
