Swimsuit? BACHIN! 💥 Towel in the water? BACHIN!! 💥💥

Onsen basics in Japan:
✅ Most onsen don't allow swimsuits
✅ Wash before you soak
✅ Keep your towel out of the water (on your head is classic)

Rules vary by facility, so check the signs or ask staff.
