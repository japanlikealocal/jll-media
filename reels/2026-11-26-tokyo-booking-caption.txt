Tokyo trip? Book these early 🎟️ Save this before you plan your days 📌

1 Ghibli Museum – tickets only in advance, none at the door. Next month goes on sale on the 10th at 10:00 Japan time. Closed most Tuesdays.
2 Tokyo Disney – buy a dated ticket first; the parks don't sell tickets at the gate. Sales open 2 months ahead at 14:00 Japan time. Check which park — closing times can differ.
3 teamLab Borderless – timed tickets. A month's dates open around the 1st, two months before. Same-day only if any are left.
4 Shibuya Sky – dated tickets open 2 weeks ahead at 0:00. If adult tickets are sold out online, they're sold out at the counter too. The rooftop can close in bad weather.
5 Tokyo Skytree – same-day tickets are fine. Quietest right after opening (weekdays around 10am, weekends around 9am); busy around 4–7pm.
6 Harry Potter Studio Tour – book a time slot ahead; popular slots sell out. Plan 3–4 hours.
7 No booking needed – Senso-ji (main hall 6:30–17:00), Meiji Jingu (November: gates close 16:10), Tsukiji Outer Market (best 9:00–14:00).

Checked Nov 22, 2026 on official sites. Hours and sale dates change — check before you go.
