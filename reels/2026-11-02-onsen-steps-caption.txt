First onsen in Japan? ♨️ 5 steps so you don't feel awkward 📌 Save this!

1 Change in the changing room — most traditional baths are without a swimsuit, separated by gender
2 Wash your whole body before you soak
3 Your small towel stays out of the water
4 Tie up long hair and keep your voice low
5 Tattoos? Ask first: "Tattoo wa daijoubu desu ka?"

Rules vary by facility — check the signs or ask staff.
