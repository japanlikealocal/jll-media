Visiting Japan from the US? 🇯🇵🇺🇸 10 everyday differences to know before you land 📌

1 Tipping – Japan: not expected · US: usual at sit-down restaurants
2 Price tags – Japan: tax included · US: sales tax often added at checkout
3 Shoes at home – Japan: off at the door · US: often kept on
4 Driving side – Japan: left · US: right
5 Escalators – Tokyo: usually stand left · US: usually stand right
6 Calls on trains – Japan: avoided on local trains · US: more common
7 Paying at restaurants – Japan: often at the register · US: often at the table
8 Greeting – Japan: often a bow · US: often a handshake
9 Hot spring bath – Japan: usually no swimsuit · US: usually a swimsuit
10 Emergency – Japan: 110 police, 119 fire/ambulance · US: 911

General customs — rules vary by place, state and business. In Japan, follow local signs and staff instructions.
