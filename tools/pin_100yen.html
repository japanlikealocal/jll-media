100-YEN SHOPS

100-Yen Shops in Japan:
5 Things to Know

1
Not always 100 yen
Many items are 100 yen plus tax,
so about 110 yen at the register.
2
Some cost more
Look for price tags. Some items
may cost 300 or 500 yen, or more.
3
Great travel goods
You can often find small bags,
adapters, umbrellas and snacks.
4
Bags may cost extra
Shopping bags often cost a few yen.
Bringing your own bag helps.
5
Check before you buy
Plugs and electronics may be made
for Japan. Check the package.
Prices vary by store. Save this for your trip!
