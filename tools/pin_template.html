TAX-FREE SHOPPING

Tax-Free Shopping Changes
from Nov 1, 2026

1
Pay the full price
Tax-free shops charge the full price,
tax included. You can get the tax back
after you leave Japan.
2
Leave within 90 days
Leave Japan within 90 days
of your purchase.
3
Check before your bags
At the airport, do the tax-free procedure
with your passport before you check in
your bags. Customs may ask to see items.
4
Keep every item
If even one item from a receipt is missing,
nothing on that receipt is refunded.
Steps may vary by airport. Check official guidance!
