LOST & FOUND

Lost Something
in Japan? 5 Things to Do

1
Ask at a koban
A koban is a small police box.
Officers can help you report a lost item.
2
Ask station staff
Lost it on a train or in a station?
Ask station staff or the lost & found.
3
Note time and place
Write down when and where you lost it,
and which train or line you used.
4
Keep taxi receipts
A taxi receipt often shows the company
name. It helps if you leave something
behind.
5
Ask your hotel
Hotel staff can often help you call
the station, police or taxi company.
Steps vary. Ask staff for help. Save this for your trip!
