LUGGAGE IN JAPAN

Too Much Luggage
in Japan?
Try These 3 Tips

1
Use coin lockers
Many stations have coin lockers.
Sizes vary, and large ones
can fill up fast.
2
Ask your hotel
Many hotels can hold bags before
check-in or after check-out.
Ask the front desk.
3
Send your suitcase
You can send suitcases to your next
hotel. Check delivery times and whether
the hotel accepts deliveries.
Rules vary by station and service.
