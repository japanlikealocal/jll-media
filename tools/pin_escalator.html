ESCALATORS

Escalators in Japan:
5 Things to Know

1
Tokyo: often left
In the Tokyo area, people often
stand on the left side.
2
Osaka: often right
In the Osaka area, people often
stand on the right side.
3
Some ask: stand still
Some stations and cities ask everyone
to stand on both sides and not walk.
4
Follow the flow
Not sure? Stand where people
in front of you are standing.
5
Hold the handrail
Keep a hand on the rail and watch
big suitcases on the steps.
Rules vary by area and station. Save this for your trip!
