FOOD ETIQUETTE

Eating While Walking
in Japan: 5 Tips

1
Eat near the stall
Many people eat food right where
they bought it.
2
Some areas ask not to
Some streets and towns have signs
asking you not to eat while walking.
3
Keep your trash
Bins can be hard to find.
Carry a small bag.
4
Konbini food
Some convenience stores have a small
eating space. Others don't.
5
Watch the crowd
On busy streets, step aside
before you eat or drink.
Rules vary by area. Follow local signs. Save this for your trip!
