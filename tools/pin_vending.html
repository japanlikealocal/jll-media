VENDING MACHINES

Vending Machines
in Japan: 5 Things to Know

1
They're very common
You'll often find them at stations,
on streets and near tourist spots.
2
Red vs. blue labels
A red label often means a hot drink,
blue often means cold. Hot drinks
usually appear in the colder months.
3
How to pay
Many take coins and 1,000-yen bills.
Some also take IC cards.
Check the machine first.
4
Grab your drink & change
Your drink usually drops into a slot
near the bottom. Check the coin return.
5
Bins for empties
Bins next to machines are usually for
empty bottles and cans only. Check the labels.
Machines vary. Save this for your trip!
