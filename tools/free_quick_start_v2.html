Japan Quick Start:
Your First Trip on One Page

Simple tips for first-time visitors. Save it on your phone or print it.

Money

  • Cards work at many hotels and big stores.
  • Keep some yen as backup. Small shops may be cash only.
  • Seven Bank ATMs accept many overseas-issued cards. Some cards may not work; fees apply.
  • NEWTax-free from Nov 1, 2026: pay the full price, then get the tax back when you leave. At the airport, do the tax-free check before checking in your bags.

Trains

  • On supported local trains, tap a funded IC card (Suica, PASMO, ICOCA) at the gates when entering and exiting. Shinkansen and limited express trains require additional tickets or arrangements.
  • Queue at the markings on the platform. Let people exit first.
  • Avoid phone calls on local trains. Keep your voice low.

Eating Out

  • Staff may ask how many people are in your group. Hold up fingers to show your group size.
  • Water and wet hand towels (oshibori) are often free.
  • Some ramen shops use ticket machines. Tipping is not expected.
  • At many restaurants, you pay at the register.

Manners

  • Remove your shoes where required, such as in homes and some traditional venues. Check signs or ask staff.
  • Trash bins are rare on streets. Carry a small bag.
  • On crowded trains, wear your backpack on the front.
