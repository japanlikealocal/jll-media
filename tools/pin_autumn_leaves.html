AUTUMN LEAVES

Autumn Leaves
in Japan: 5 Things to Know

1
North first, south later
Leaves usually turn first in the north
and in the mountains, then spread
south and into city parks.
2
A long season
About late September to early December,
depending on place and elevation.
3
Tokyo & Osaka
The 2026 forecast puts Tokyo around
late November and Osaka around
early December.
4
Check the forecast
Forecasts can change with the weather.
Check the latest one before you go.
5
Go early
Popular spots can get very busy.
Early mornings are often calmer.
Timing varies every year. Save this for your trip!
