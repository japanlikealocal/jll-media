BOWING

Do You Need to Bow
in Japan? 5 Tips

1
A small nod is fine
Visitors are not expected to bow
perfectly. A small nod often works.
2
Say thank you
"Arigatou gozaimasu" with a smile
goes a long way.
3
Staff may bow to you
Shop staff often bow. A nod or
a smile back is a kind reply.
4
Handshakes happen too
Some people may offer a handshake,
especially in business. Follow their lead.
5
Don't overthink it
Being polite matters more than
the exact angle of your bow.
Customs vary by situation. Save this for your trip!
