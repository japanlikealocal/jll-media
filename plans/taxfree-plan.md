# Reel 7 – Tax-free shopping change (final plan)

## Slides

0 (hook)
- Label: TAX-FREE SHOPPING
- Title: Shopping tax-free in Japan from Nov 1, 2026?
- Sub: 3 changes to know 👇

1 – Pay the full price
From Nov 1, 2026, tax-free shops charge the full price, tax included.
You can get the tax back after you leave Japan.

2 – Do the customs check first
Leave Japan within 90 days of purchase.
At the airport, do the tax-free check with your passport BEFORE you check in your bags.
Customs may ask to see your items.

3 – Keep every item
If even one item from a receipt is missing, you get no refund for anything on that receipt.

End
