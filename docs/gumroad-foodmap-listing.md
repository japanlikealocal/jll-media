## Name
Tokyo Food Map: 48 Local Spots — Gyoza, Ramen, Unagi & Yakitori (2026)

## Description
Hungry in Tokyo but not sure where to go — or whether they’ll take your card?

This 21-page PDF gives you 48 food spots across Tokyo, 12 each for gyoza, ramen, unagi (grilled eel) and yakitori. For every spot you get:

• The Japanese name to show staff
• Nearest station and walk time
• What to order, with prices where the shop publishes them
• Cash or card — clearly marked
• Booking rules (walk-in, online booking, timed tickets)
• English menu: yes, online only, or unknown
• Opening days where published
• A one-tap Google Maps link

Also inside: an area index (find food near your hotel), how ordering works at each kind of shop, 8 point-and-say Japanese phrases, and a mini menu dictionary.

Facts were checked against each shop’s official page or restaurant listing between Oct 29 and Nov 2, 2026. Shops change hours and prices, so check on the day.

Good to know
